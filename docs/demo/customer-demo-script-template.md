## The narrative arc

### 1. Hook (60 sec)
Open with their pain, in their words: "[persona] teams spend X hours on Y…"
State the promise: "In the next ten minutes I'll show you how [product] turns
that into [outcome]."

### 2. Context (60 sec)
One screen or sentence on what the product is. No architecture. No feature tour.

### 3. Hero moment (3–4 min)
The single most valuable flow, shown live:
- Step: … → what to say: …
- Step: … → what to say: …
- **The moment**: [the screen/result that makes them lean in] → pause here.

### 4. Proof and depth (2–3 min)
Answer the skeptic: data quality, security posture, how it fits their stack
(Azure-friendly is often a selling point). Show one supporting feature max.

### 5. Outcome recap (60 sec)
Restate value in their metric: time saved, risk reduced, revenue found.

### 6. The ask (60 sec)
Concrete next step: pilot proposal, follow-up with their team, data sample.
"[Specific ask] — does [timeframe] work?"
