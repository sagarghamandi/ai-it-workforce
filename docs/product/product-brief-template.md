# Product Brief — [Product Name]

*Owner: [human product owner] · Drafted by: [agent role] · Date: · Status: Draft / Approved*

## Problem
What problem, for whom, and how painful is it today? (2–4 sentences, customer language.)

## Target customer
Who buys, who uses, and what segment/size? Name the persona(s).

## Proposed solution
One paragraph: what we build and the single "hero moment" it delivers.

## Why now / why us
Market timing, unfair advantage, or credibility angle. (Federal/enterprise angle if relevant.)

## Demo value
What does the 10-minute demo show that makes a buyer lean in?

## Monetization hypothesis
Who pays, roughly what, in what model (pilot fee, subscription, usage)?

## MVP sketch
3–5 bullet capabilities. Everything else is "Later".

## Out of scope (explicitly)
What we are NOT building in this phase.

## Success criteria
How we'll know this was worth building (demo booked, pilot signed, N users to value).

## Risks and open questions
Top 3 with a note on how we'll resolve each.
